# FlowersApps 🌸

FlowersApps develops software solutions designed to improve quality control, logistics, traceability, reporting, and operational processes across the flower industry.

Our goal is to transform field and operational workflows into reliable digital processes, connecting teams, data, and decision-making through practical and scalable technology.

## What we build

- Quality control and inspection applications
- Farm and cargo agency workflows
- Postharvest and flower evaluation systems
- Traceability and operational management tools
- Inventory and material management
- Reporting and business intelligence solutions
- Web platforms, APIs, and mobile applications
- Integrations between operational systems and data platforms

## Technology

Our ecosystem includes mobile, web, backend, database, reporting, and cloud technologies, with solutions built around real operational needs.

We focus on:

- Reliability
- Traceability
- Simple user experiences
- Data-driven decision making
- Process automation
- Scalable software architecture

## Our approach

We build technology close to the operation.

From inspections in farms and cargo agencies to management dashboards and reporting platforms, FlowersApps focuses on creating tools that simplify processes while maintaining accurate and traceable information.
